# denominator for every weight tensor all through training, so the updates are SGD at lr / eps, and layers with
# small gradients learn slowest (at epoch 5 the second layer's recurrent weights have a gradient RMS of 5e-4, the

#   P_noise05     white noise sd 0.5 instead of 1.0 (the RNN's 1.0 is cut ~2.7x by its input smoothing; ours is not)
